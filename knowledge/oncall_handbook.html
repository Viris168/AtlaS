On-call Handbook

This handbook explains how the platform team handles incidents outside office hours. Every engineer joins the rotation after their first three months.

Rotation

Shifts last one week and hand over every Monday at 10:00. The primary on-call engineer carries the pager; the secondary takes over if the primary does not acknowledge a page within 10 minutes.

Swaps are fine: agree with a teammate and update the schedule in the paging tool before the shift starts. Each on-call week earns one day off, to be taken within the next month.

Severity levels

LevelExampleResponse
SEV1Checkout is down for all customersAcknowledge within 5 minutes, all hands, status page updated every 30 minutes
SEV2One region or one major feature is degradedAcknowledge within 15 minutes, work it during the night
SEV3A minor bug with a workaroundTicket for the next working day, no page

Escalation

If you cannot make progress on a SEV1 within 30 minutes, page the engineering manager on duty. For anything touching customer data or payments, also page the security lead, whatever the time.

The first engineer to acknowledge a SEV1 becomes the incident commander until they hand the role over explicitly. The commander coordinates and communicates; they should not also be the one typing fixes.

Deploy freeze

Freeze windows

No production deploys on Fridays after 15:00, on public holidays, or between December 20 and January 3. Scheduled jobs and config changes count as deploys.

Exceptions

A SEV1 hotfix may ship during a freeze with written approval from the incident commander in the incident channel. Roll forward only; never use a freeze exception for new features.

After an incident

Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a blameless postmortem within five working days: a timeline, the root cause, what went well, and action items with owners. Postmortems are shared with the whole engineering team.
